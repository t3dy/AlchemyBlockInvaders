FOUR ALCHEMICAL GAMES

Alchemy is a system of correspondences: fire does one thing to water and another to earth, gold answers to nothing but itself, the volatile must be fixed before it is any use to you. Those correspondences are rules — and the honest way to learn a rule is to play a game whose outcome depends on it.

So there are four games here, in four different genres, each turning a different part of the tradition into its mechanics. Nothing is behind a tutorial and nothing is locked. Play them in any order.

All four run in the browser with no install and no account. Three are keyboard games; the first also takes touch. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — progress lives in the page and goes away when you close it.

THE GAMES

🜂 ALCHEMY BLOCK INVADERS SHOOTER · THE REACTION TABLE

the blocks are matter, and your shot has a kind

Space Invaders in which every descending block is an element or a planet, and so is the shot you answer it with. What happens when they meet is not damage — it is the reaction between those two kinds of matter, and it is different for every pair. You pick a glyph before you begin; it becomes your shield and bends your affinities. Each reaction you discover is written into the Tome, which is the game's real scorecard.

WHAT IT IS FOR — learning the reaction table by consequence rather than by recitation. You are never told that water slakes calx; you find out because it is the thing that worked.

✷ NOVA HEAT DEFENCE · SACRED GEOMETRY

Fruit of Life · thirteen circles · seventy-eight lines

Metatron's Cube, drawn in pencil, holding thirteen circles. Place spheres of prana into them; any two charged circles light the line between them, and a lit line burns. The Nova Mob comes in from the margin to drain the figure and reach the centre, and every one that arrives scorches the paper. Hold seven circles or more and the paper cools. Light all thirteen and the Mer-Ka-Ba turns — it clears the page, but it is built out of the figure and leaves every circle dark behind it.

WHAT IT IS FOR — the figure as a graph. Its meaning is not the picture but which points are joined to which, and the game is unplayable until you start seeing it that way. The Burroughs cut-up is in here as a weapon, because the counter-move against a machine that keeps the tape in order is to take scissors to it.

THE SKILL — place on the peak of the inhale, the dashed window on the breath meter, and the sphere costs 9 prana instead of 16 and lands at 135%. That timing is the whole game.

☿ CIRCULATIO BOUNCE · PHYSICS AS METAPHYSICS

the matter rises and falls until it is fixed

A bounce-and-strike course in the manner of Kirby's Dream Course, except that the physics is a philosophy and you may change it mid-round. Aristotelian bodies seek their natural place: heavy gravity, dead rebounds, ground that grips. The Paracelsian, Pythagorean and Lettrist readings each answer differently. You are the matter in the vessel; every body you touch is dissolved, and when one remains it becomes the mouth of the flask and you must fall into it.

WHAT IT IS FOR — making the argument that a physics is a commitment about what the world is like, not a neutral description of it. The same course, the same shot, four incompatible worlds. Each ruleset names its source on screen.

🜍 SALAMANDRA FLIGHT · THE MATRIX AS ARCHITECTURE

a flight through the burning body

You fly an alembic through the inside of the athanor, and the furnace is built. Every structure in there is masonry of a particular alchemical kind and it answers to one thing only. Your gun is not one gun but eleven kinds of matter, and the wrong one is absorbed, or hardens the wall, or makes it grow, or jets flame down the lane, or is thrown straight back at you. At the far end stands the solar lock, which is gold, and gold answers to nothing but itself. Open a lane through it and fly out.

WHAT IT IS FOR — the reaction table again, but as terrain rather than as a table. You cannot shoot your way through this by reflex; you have to read what the wall is made of and load the answer to it.

Four separate games sharing one repository. Each is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies except three.js, which Circulatio uses to draw its courses.

The design notes, the reaction tables and the record of what has actually been tested are kept alongside the code in DESIGN.md, TOME_ENTRIES.md and VERIFIED.md.
