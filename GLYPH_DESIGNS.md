# Glyph Designs — Five Options Per Symbol
## Alchemy Balance Tetris & Alchemy Block Invaders
### Design Review Document

---

## HOW TO READ THIS DOCUMENT

Each glyph has five numbered designs. Each design has:

- **Name** — The mechanic concept
- **Invaders** — How it manifests when you *shoot* this block
- **Tetris** — How it manifests when this piece *lands or clears*
- **Tome Entry** — The first-person knowledge unlock the player reads
- **Cross-game coherence** — Does the metaphor hold in both games?

At the end, a **Coherence Audit** rates which designs best teach alchemy consistently.

## THE FOUR ELEMENTS

---

### 🔥 FIRE

**Core alchemical identity:** Transformation, ascent, appetite, purification by destruction, Sulfur affinity, masculine, hot & dry.

---

**Design 1 — Combustion**
- **Invaders:** When shot, Fire block explodes and deals splash damage to all blocks in a 2-block radius. If those blocks are also Fire, they chain-explode. Water blocks in radius are converted to Steam (new block type, lighter, different behavior).
- **Tetris:** When a Fire piece lands, all blocks directly below it (same column, touching) are removed instantly — "burned through." This clears partial lines but can create gaps.
- **Tome Entry:** "You struck the fire block and it consumed what surrounded it. Fire does not spread: it *hungers*. Note that it cannot burn what is already burning — fire recognizes fire."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Fire's destructive appetite holds in both contexts. Both use "burning through" as the core metaphor.

---

**Design 2 — Ascension**
- **Invaders:** Fire block, when shot, rises rather than exploding downward — it launches upward off-screen, leaving a trail that damages any blocks directly above the point of impact.
- **Tetris:** A Fire piece, when placed, causes all Fire pieces on the board to shift one cell upward (they float). If they float off the top, game threatens — but if they're still on the board, they leave their column one row lighter.
- **Tome Entry:** "Fire moves upward always. Even destroyed, it escapes toward the sky. An alchemist learns to direct this ascent rather than fight it — the work is to give fire a path, not a cage."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Fire's upward nature (sulphuric volatility) is mechanically real in both games.

---

**Design 3 — Illumination**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Fire reveals the true element of all blocks within 3 rows of the shot — hidden blocks (unlabeled ones) become visible. Fire "illuminates" what was dark.
- **Tetris:** When a Fire piece clears a line, it reveals the "nature" of every block in the rows above — each block shows its affinity briefly (glows its elemental color). Knowledge is unlocked for one turn.
- **Tome Entry:** "Fire is the first revealer. Before the microscope, before the assay, fire showed the alchemist what a substance was made of by what it released. Heat strips pretense."
- **Coherence:** ✓ A more unusual read — Fire as epistemological tool rather than weapon.

---

**Design 4 — Calcination**
- **Invaders:** Fire block, when destroyed, leaves an "Ash" remnant block in its place. Ash is brittle (one more hit destroys it), but hitting Ash with Earth creates Soil — a bonus block with special properties.
- **Tetris:** Fire pieces, when they land, gradually calcinate (color shifts toward grey over 5 turns). Calcinated pieces are worth double points when cleared, but if too many calcinate without being cleared, the board "cools" and game slows.
- **Tome Entry:** "Calcination is the First Work. Fire reduces the body to dry powder — ash — removing the moisture of delusion. What remains cannot pretend to be anything other than what it is."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Residue/transformation idea works in both. Strongly alchemical (Calcination = Aries process).

---

**Design 5 — Fermentation**
- **Invaders:** A Fire block that's been on screen for 3+ seconds without being shot begins to pulse and grow — it ferments. Fermented Fire blocks spawn a secondary small fire block each turn. Destroying them before fermentation grants bonus XP.
- **Tetris:** Fire pieces that sit on the board without being cleared for 5+ turns enter Fermentation — they begin attracting other Fire pieces toward them (slight gravity). This helps or hinders depending on build strategy.
- **Tome Entry:** "Fire is the agent of fermentation. Without heat, wine remains grape juice, bread remains dough, cheese remains milk. The alchemist maintains a precise, patient fire — hot enough to transform, not enough to destroy."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Leo/Fermentation correspondence is explicit and teaches the zodiac link.

### 💧 WATER

**Core alchemical identity:** Dissolution, flow, reflection, the lunar/feminine principle, cold & wet, solvency, the prima materia state.

---

**Design 1 — Dissolution**
- **Invaders:** Water block, when shot, dissolves into a spreading liquid that flows downward across 3 cells. Blocks caught in the flow are weakened (take double damage from next hit). Earth blocks caught in Water flow become Mud — slower, heavier.
- **Tetris:** A Water piece landing on top of other pieces causes them to "dissolve" slightly — those pieces shift and settle into gaps they wouldn't otherwise fill. Gaps one cell wide are automatically filled by Water's dissolution.
- **Tome Entry:** "Water does not strike — it seeps. Given enough time, water dissolves stone. The alchemist uses water's patience as a tool: dissolution precedes all recombination. You cannot join what has not first been separated."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Gap-filling in Tetris perfectly mirrors dissolution's gap-finding.

---

**Design 2 — Cohobation (Recycling)**
- **Invaders:** Water blocks, when shot, don't disappear — they respawn at the top of the screen after 5 seconds. This represents water's cyclical nature (evaporation/precipitation). The player must shoot them again.
- **Tetris:** When a Water piece clears a line, one of the blocks from that line is returned to the queue as a bonus piece. Water "recycles" what is used.
- **Tome Entry:** "Cohobation: redistillation over the same matter. Water is not consumed — it is cycled. The alchemist returns the distillate to its base repeatedly, each pass refining what remains. Water teaches that nothing is truly lost."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Cycling/return mechanic holds in both games. Strong alchemical teaching.

---

**Design 3 — Reflection**
- **Invaders:** The Water block acts as a mirror when shot — the bullet reflects and travels a second trajectory. If that reflected bullet hits another Water block, it reflects again. Chain reflections score multiplied XP.
- **Tetris:** Water pieces, when placed adjacent to any other piece, "reflect" its elemental property for the purpose of scoring — e.g., Water next to Fire scores as Fire+Water combination, unlocking that reaction entry.
- **Tome Entry:** "The Moon rules water, and the Moon is a mirror. Water reflects what approaches it. The alchemist who works with water must examine what they see reflected, for water shows truth without distortion — unlike fire, which illuminates by consuming."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Reflection as a physical and philosophical mechanic. Mirror/Moon connection is solid.

---

**Design 4 — Accumulation**
- **Invaders:** Water blocks are immune to single shots. They require two hits to destroy. On first hit they expand — growing from 1 cell to 4 cells (2×2). The expanded block is then a big target but also blocks more of the field.
- **Tetris:** Water pieces accumulate — each Water piece placed adds to a "Water Level." At 5 Water pieces, all Water pieces on the board merge into a single large block (2×2) that counts as a line anchor, drawing surrounding pieces in slightly.
- **Tome Entry:** "Water accumulates. One drop is nothing; a river reshapes continents. The alchemist learns to work with water's patience: add slowly, observe carefully, and expect transformation to arrive gradually and irreversibly."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Accumulation-before-power is a satisfying Tetris teaching that maps to alchemical patience.

---

**Design 5 — Solvent**
- **Invaders:** Shooting a Water block causes it to release a "solvent cloud" that, on next turn, changes the element of all adjacent blocks to a hybrid (Fire+Water = Steam, Earth+Water = Mud, Air+Water = Mist). Each hybrid has different properties and unlocks cascade Tome entries.
- **Tetris:** When a Water piece is placed adjacent to any non-Water piece, both pieces gain a "combined" glow and score double when cleared together. Clears of combined pairs unlock cascade Tome entries.
- **Tome Entry:** "The Alchemical Solvent dissolves the boundaries between things. This is water's deepest secret: it does not merely mix with other substances — it opens them, renders them receptive to transformation they would otherwise resist."
- **Coherence:** ✓ The cascade Tome unlock mechanic works especially well here — Water's solvent nature as the enabler of all other reactions.

---

### 🌬️ AIR

**Core alchemical identity:** Dispersion, mediation, volatility, Mercury affinity, the carrier of essences, light & rising, Gemini/Separation process.

---

**Design 1 — Dispersion**
- **Invaders:** Air block, when shot, doesn't stay destroyed — it disperses into 4 small "air motes" that travel horizontally outward. Each mote that hits another block weakens it (half health). Motes hit everything in a row.
- **Tetris:** An Air piece, when it lands, "disperses" — each individual block in the piece flies to the leftmost/rightmost empty cell in its row, spreading maximally. This helps fill partial rows but breaks up deliberate stacking.
- **Tome Entry:** "Air disperses. Strike it and it becomes smaller things in more places. The alchemist who attempts to capture air in a vessel discovers that the experiment has already failed — air negotiates with containers, not the reverse."
- **Coherence:** ✓ The dispersion mechanic (concentrating vs. dispersing) creates tension in both games.

---

**Design 2 — Volatilization**
- **Invaders:** Air blocks are fast — they move faster than other blocks (1.5× speed). When shot, they leave a "volatile trail" that temporarily amplifies the next bullet that passes through it (double damage).
- **Tetris:** Air pieces fall faster than other pieces (1.5× drop rate). However, they clear a "volatile line" — an Air line clear gives +50% score multiplier to the next line cleared after it.
- **Tome Entry:** "Volatilization is the process of becoming gaseous, becoming mobile, becoming swift. What is volatile rises fastest, disperses furthest, reacts soonest. The alchemist praises volatility in substances and cultivates it in herself."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Speed/volatility is mechanically coherent. The "amplifier" property maps well.

---

**Design 3 — Separation**
- **Invaders:** Air block, when shot, causes all blocks in its row to momentarily separate — gap opens between each block for one turn, making them individually targetable. Mass-targeting opportunity (Gemini/Separation process).
- **Tetris:** Air pieces, when part of a completed line, "separate" the line before clearing — briefly showing each component element glowing its true color, then clearing. This unlocks Tome entries for every element present in that line.
- **Tome Entry:** "Separation is the third operation of the Great Work. You scatter what was mixed to discover what was combined. Only by separating fire from water, mercury from salt, can you know what you are working with. Air is the separator."
- **Coherence:** ✓ "Separation as knowledge" is perfect Tome unlock logic — seeing what's in a line.

---

**Design 4 — Carrier**
- **Invaders:** Air blocks act as "carriers" — on the turn they're spawned, they carry a secondary element (randomly chosen). Shooting Air first releases the carrier element as a separate block. Players learn to read what Air carries before shooting.
- **Tetris:** Air pieces "carry" another element — visible as a small icon overlay. When placed, the carried element spawns as an additional single-cell block wherever Air connects. Unexpected bonuses, unexpected complications.
- **Tome Entry:** "Air carries what it passes through. Smoke carries fire's residue. Wind carries seeds. The alchemist uses bellows not merely for heat but for transport — air moves the subtle essences from one operation to another. Watch what air brings."
- **Coherence:** ✓ The "carrier" concept is deep and teaches air's role in alchemical process correctly.

---

**Design 5 — Levity**
- **Invaders:** Air blocks drift upward (against gravity) and must be shot before reaching the top of the screen, where they escape. A missed Air block provides a XP penalty — the alchemist "lost" the knowledge.
- **Tetris:** Air pieces want to float — when placed, they slowly rise one cell per turn unless "anchored" by adjacent Earth or Saturn blocks. Players must deliberately cage Air pieces. A properly anchored Air piece scores bonus points.
- **Tome Entry:** "Levity is air's fundamental property, as gravity is earth's. The alchemist who ignores levity loses their volatile essences to the sky. To work with air is to work with what refuses to stay still — the restless, the rising, the escaped."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Upward-drift tension (you must chase or contain Air) is a distinctive mechanic that maps to both contexts.

---

### 🌍 EARTH

**Core alchemical identity:** Stability, fixation, heaviness, the body/vessel, Salt affinity, cold & dry, Capricorn/Fixation process.

---

**Design 1 — Fixation**
- **Invaders:** Earth blocks are slow-moving but act as anchors — other blocks caught in Earth's path slow down as they approach (gravity increase in proximity). Earth blocks require 2 hits to destroy.
- **Tetris:** Earth pieces, when placed, "fix" all adjacent pieces — they cannot be rotated or moved by future piece interactions (Air dispersion, Water dissolution) while they remain. Earth pieces are permanent anchors.
- **Tome Entry:** "Fixation is the art of making permanent. The alchemist fixes the volatile — captures what would escape — using salts and earths and cold stone. What is fixed cannot flee. What is fixed can be worked with indefinitely."
- **Coherence:** ✓ "Anchor" mechanic is the clearest possible teaching of Earth's alchemical role.

---

**Design 2 — Crystallization**
- **Invaders:** Earth blocks spawn as single cells but grow — every 3 seconds they add a cell in a random direction (crystallizing). Fully crystallized Earth becomes a 3×3 mass requiring many hits. Early destruction is rewarded.
- **Tetris:** Earth pieces "crystallize" over time — after 4 turns on the board, they expand by one cell in each direction they have empty space. Crystallized Earth is worth double when cleared but can fill the board dangerously.
- **Tome Entry:** "The mineral world grows by crystallization — the patient accretion of layer upon layer, following invisible templates. The alchemist studies crystals to understand how form emerges from formlessness. Earth builds; it does not create spontaneously."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Crystallization growth creates pressure in both games that teaches Earth's property through gameplay pressure.

---

**Design 3 — Vessel**
- **Invaders:** Earth blocks act as containers — when near (within 2 cells) of another element, they "absorb" one of its properties, slowing and containing it. Shooting a Vessel Earth block releases what it absorbed as a cascade effect.
- **Tetris:** Earth pieces act as vessels — when placed adjacent to any other element, they "hold" that element's bonus property. A 3-piece Earth-Fire-Earth sequence creates a Crucible — a special combo worth triple points.
- **Tome Entry:** "The vessel is the alchemist's most important tool. Without containment, there is no transformation — only dissipation. Earth provides the vessel in nature: the stone retort, the clay flask, the mineral matrix. Honor the container."
- **Coherence:** ✓ "Vessel" mechanic (containing other elements' power) teaches Earth's Paracelsian role as Salt/Body perfectly.

---

**Design 4 — Precipitation**
- **Invaders:** Earth blocks always fall faster than other blocks. When they hit the "ground" (bottom of screen), they don't disappear — they stack as permanent obstacles that reduce the playable area. Must be "mined" by 3 consecutive shots.
- **Tetris:** Earth pieces fall 2× faster than other pieces. When multiple Earth pieces land in the same column, that column becomes a "Foundation" — adjacent pieces landing near it score +25%.
- **Tome Entry:** "Earth precipitates from solution. When water evaporates or cools, what was held in suspension becomes solid — precipitate, sediment, ore. The alchemist learns to control precipitation: too fast and the crystal is crude; too slow and it never forms."
- **Coherence:** ✓ Fast-fall teaches heaviness; bonus for foundation teaches Earth's structural role.

---

**Design 5 — Materia Prima**
- **Invaders:** Earth blocks are the raw material state — they can be transformed by adjacent element blocks. Earth near Fire becomes calcinated (grey, brittle). Earth near Water becomes Mud. Earth near Air becomes Dust. Each transformation unlocks a Tome entry.
- **Tetris:** Earth pieces are "unformed" — they visually show a grey/brown question mark. When placed adjacent to another element, they transform into that element's corresponding material (Earth+Fire = Ash, Earth+Water = Clay, Earth+Air = Sand). Each creates a unique combo bonus.
- **Tome Entry:** "Aristotle called earth the first matter — materia prima — the substrate on which all other principles act. Earth alone is inert. Earth transformed by other elements becomes everything: ore, clay, sand, ash, soil. Earth is potential before it is substance."
- **Coherence:** ✓ The "transformation receptor" mechanic makes Earth a hub for interactions, incentivizing players to try all combinations.

## THE SEVEN PLANETS

---

### ☉ SUN (Gold)

**Core identity:** Perfection, the goal of the Great Work, Sol, the masculine solar principle, gold, completion, the highest state.

---

**Design 1 — Purification**
- **Invaders:** Sun blocks radiate — any block within 2 cells slowly loses its impurities (special status effects are removed). Shooting Sun blocks destroys them but releases a "golden burst" that purifies all blocks on screen simultaneously, removing negative modifiers.
- **Tetris:** When a Sun piece lands, all pieces currently affected by negative modifiers (Frozen by Saturn, Muddied by Water+Earth, etc.) are cleansed. The Sun line clears always count double.
- **Tome Entry:** "Gold is perfect because it has survived all purifications. Fire cannot consume it; acid can only dissolve it, not destroy it. To transmute lead to gold was to purify — to remove all that was not already perfect. The sun reveals what purity means."

---

**Design 2 — Amplification**
- **Invaders:** Sun blocks pulse — each pulse (every 2 seconds) amplifies all blocks in the row by +1 strength. Shooting a Sun block before 3 pulses releases stored amplitude as a score bonus.
- **Tetris:** Sun pieces, when included in a cleared line, multiply that line's score by the number of Sun pieces present (1 Sun = 2×, 2 Suns = 4×). Maximum amplification.

---

**Design 3 — Attraction**
- **Invaders:** Sun blocks attract all bullet trajectories slightly — bullets fired within 3 cells curve toward the Sun block. Skilled players can use this for ricochet shots.
- **Tetris:** Sun pieces attract adjacent pieces — when placed, the two closest pieces in the same row shift one cell toward the Sun piece. Useful for filling gaps, dangerous if Sun is misplaced.

---

**Design 4 — Completion**
- **Invaders:** Sun blocks are only destructible with the 7th shot in a row without missing. Any miss resets the count. The Sun block is the "boss" of each wave.
- **Tetris:** Sun pieces can only be placed in the bottom-most available position — they always drop to the lowest point. Once Sun reaches the bottom row, it scores a "Great Work" bonus clearing all completed Sun sequences.

---

**Design 5 — Illumination of Secrets**
- **Invaders:** Shooting a Sun block reveals all hidden reaction potentials for 5 seconds — every block on screen shows what it would produce if combined with the adjacent blocks.
- **Tetris:** Sun pieces, when cleared, reveal the "ideal placement" for the next 3 pieces — ghost pieces show where they should go for maximum XP. The Sun illuminates the path.
- **Tome Entry (all Solar designs share):** "The philosophers called gold Sol — the Sun made tangible. The goal of the Great Work was not wealth but perfection: the discovery that what appeared base was gold all along, obscured by impurity. To achieve Sol is to achieve completion."

---

### ☽ MOON (Silver)

**Core identity:** Reflection, the feminine principle, Luna, silver, cycles, purification through repetition, albedo (whitening phase).

---

**Design 1 — Mirroring**
- **Invaders:** Moon blocks mirror all bullets — a bullet hitting a Moon block reflects perfectly back toward the player's vessel. Players must dodge their own reflected shots.
- **Tetris:** Moon pieces mirror the next piece — when Moon lands, the upcoming piece in queue becomes a mirror (horizontally flipped) version of itself.

---

**Design 2 — Cycles**
- **Invaders:** Moon blocks have phases — they cycle through Full (large, 2 cells), Half (1 cell), Crescent (half-cell, partially transparent), and back. Only destructible at Full phase.
- **Tetris:** Moon pieces cycle through phases as they fall. At Full Moon phase (every 4 turns), cleared Moon lines score triple. Players must time their Moon clears.

---

**Design 3 — Tides**
- **Invaders:** Moon blocks influence the "gravity" of nearby blocks — blocks within 2 cells oscillate left and right (tidal pulling) rather than descending straight. Makes targeting harder; the pull is predictable.
- **Tetris:** Moon pieces cause nearby pieces to drift toward the Moon piece's column (tidal gravity). Creates compression effects that help or hinder depending on strategy.

---

**Design 4 — Albedo (Whitening)**
- **Invaders:** Moon blocks, when shot, "whiten" all adjacent blocks — converting them to a silvery neutral state with no elemental property. Whitened blocks are easy to destroy (+1 shot kills) but give no XP.
- **Tetris:** Moon pieces "whiten" their row — all blocks in the row are neutralized when Moon lands there. This is both helpful (simplifying complex situations) and costly (losing elemental bonuses).

---

**Design 5 — Receptivity**
- **Invaders:** Moon blocks absorb and store the element of the bullet that destroys them. If shot with Fire, they become a Fire Moon — which then fires back a Fire blast. The player chooses how to charge the Moon.
- **Tetris:** Moon pieces are blank — they take the elemental identity of whatever piece they land adjacent to (most recently placed). A Moon next to Fire becomes a Fire Moon; clears use the adopted identity.
- **Tome Entry (all Lunar designs):** "Luna is the mirror, the receiver, the vessel of reflection. Silver is softer than gold, and its softness is its power — silver yields, accepts, reflects. The alchemist who attempts to force the Moon discovers they can only align with it. Luna's lesson is receptivity."

---

### ☿ MERCURY (Quicksilver)

**Core identity:** Mediation, the trickster, the messenger between extremes, Hermes, volatility, transformation itself, both male and female, the Philosopher's Mercury.

---

**Design 1 — Unpredictability**
- **Invaders:** Mercury blocks teleport — they don't descend in a straight line but jump randomly within 2 cells each turn. Players must track and anticipate.
- **Tetris:** Mercury pieces, after landing, may randomly shift one cell in any direction if adjacent to certain elements (Fire moves them upward, Earth moves them down, Water shifts sideways).

---

**Design 2 — Hermetic Mediation**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Mercury releases a "mediation field" — all elements within 3 cells are temporarily neutral (their special effects suspended for 2 turns). Mercury creates a quiet zone.
- **Tetris:** Mercury pieces placed between two different elements create a "Hermetic Bridge" — the two flanking pieces interact as if adjacent, even if Mercury is between them. Enables combos impossible otherwise.

---

**Design 3 — Transmutation**
- **Invaders:** Mercury blocks randomly change element type every 3 seconds (cycling through all 7 types). Shooting them mid-cycle gives different results depending on what they currently are.
- **Tetris:** Mercury pieces transform one turn after landing — they randomly become one of the other 6 piece types. Players see a countdown (3-2-1-Transmute). What it becomes affects their strategy.

---

**Design 4 — Communication/Chain**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Mercury creates a chain — all other Mercury blocks on screen are affected simultaneously (weakened or transformed), as Mercury communicates across any distance.
- **Tetris:** Mercury pieces form chains — any two Mercury pieces on the board are "linked." When one clears, the other also clears immediately (regardless of whether its row is complete).

---

**Design 5 — Amalgamation**
- **Invaders:** Mercury blocks merge with adjacent blocks — if a Mercury block touches any other element, they amalgamate (combine), becoming a hybrid block requiring 2 specialized hits.
- **Tetris:** Mercury pieces amalgamate with whatever they land on — merging with the piece below them into a 2-high hybrid. Hybrid pieces have combined properties of both elements.
- **Tome Entry (all Mercury designs):** "Mercury is neither male nor female, neither fixed nor volatile, neither element nor compound. Mercury is the third thing — the mediator between Sulfur and Salt, the messenger between fire and water, the agent of transmutation itself. Without Mercury, Sulfur and Salt cannot meet."

### ♀ VENUS (Copper)

**Core identity:** Love, harmony, beauty, generation, fertility, the green lion of alchemy, vitalization, Taurus/Libra ruler.

---

**Design 1 — Attraction/Harmony**
- **Invaders:** Venus blocks attract nearby blocks toward them — blocks within 3 cells drift toward Venus. This clusters enemies, making group shots possible but also more dangerous.
- **Tetris:** Venus pieces, when placed, attract all same-color blocks one cell closer. Creates harmony clusters that score bonus points for proximity.

---

**Design 2 — Generation (Multiplication)**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Venus releases seeds — 3 new small blocks spawn at random positions. These secondary blocks are weaker but increase the field density.
- **Tetris:** Venus pieces, when part of a cleared line, "generate" one additional piece added to the queue — a "gift" piece. Repeated Venus clears build up bonus pieces.

---

**Design 3 — Beauty (Aesthetic Bonus)**
- **Invaders:** Venus blocks grant XP bonus for elegant shots — shooting multiple blocks in a visually symmetrical pattern doubles the XP. Venus rewards beauty in play.
- **Tetris:** Venus pieces score bonus points based on the symmetry of the board at the moment they land — more symmetric board = more Venus bonus. Encourages aesthetic board management.

---

**Design 4 — Green Lion (Vitriol)**
- **Invaders:** Venus blocks release Green Vitriol (acid) when shot — the acid stream flows downward, dissolving blocks below the point of impact. Corrodes Earth and Metal blocks but is neutralized by Water.
- **Tetris:** Venus pieces have acid underbelly — all blocks directly below a Venus piece, when Venus clears, are weakened (require an extra line to clear normally).

---

**Design 5 — Conjunction (Union)**
- **Invaders:** Two Venus blocks placed in proximity automatically conjoin — they become one larger block that, when destroyed, releases both their properties simultaneously as a combined reaction.
- **Tetris:** Venus pieces placed adjacent to another piece create a Conjunction — the two pieces fuse visually and score double when cleared together (must clear in same line clear).
- **Tome Entry (all Venus designs):** "Venus governs copper and copper governs love. The alchemists encoded gender into metals: gold is male, silver female, and copper is their union — generation itself. Venus is the principle of joining, the attraction that makes combination possible. Without Venus, elements remain apart."

---

### ♂ MARS (Iron)

**Core identity:** War, force, dissolution through violence, the sword of calcination, masculine aggression, Aries ruler, Iron.

---

**Design 1 — Ignition (Sparks)**
- **Invaders:** Mars blocks, when shot, release sparks — projectiles that travel diagonally in both directions, potentially hitting blocks in adjacent columns (unintended damage).
- **Tetris:** Mars pieces, when placed adjacent to Fire pieces, ignite — both blocks glow and score triple points when cleared. Mars activates Fire's full potential.

---

**Design 2 — Fortification**
- **Invaders:** Mars blocks serve as shields — blocks directly behind a Mars block require hitting Mars first. Mars protects whatever follows it.
- **Tetris:** Mars pieces, when placed, fortify adjacent pieces — they cannot be dissolved by Water or dispersed by Air. Mars hardens what it touches.

---

**Design 3 — Dissolution by Force**
- **Invaders:** Mars blocks are immune to normal shots but shatter when hit by a combo — 3 blocks destroyed in quick succession (under 1 second) generates a "force wave" that destroys Mars.
- **Tetris:** Mars pieces can only be cleared by force clears (4-line Tetris). Single and double line clears leave Mars in place as an obstacle, requiring more aggressive stacking.

---

**Design 4 — Rust (Decay Over Time)**
- **Invaders:** Mars blocks start red and strong. Over 10 seconds, they rust (color fades, weaken). Fully rusted Mars blocks crumble on their own — no shot required. Patience defeats Mars.
- **Tetris:** Mars pieces rust over 8 turns — color changes from red → brown → grey. Rusted Mars pieces clear for half points. Players must clear Mars before it rusts fully.

---

**Design 5 — The Sword (Cutting Lines)**
- **Invaders:** Mars blocks, when shot, release a "sword" projectile — a straight horizontal line that damages all blocks in the same row simultaneously (like a sweep).
- **Tetris:** Mars pieces are "cutting" — when placed, they clear the row they occupy of all blocks, but score nothing. Mars sacrifices points for board control.
- **Tome Entry (all Mars designs):** "Iron is the metal of Mars, and Mars the god of dissolution by force. The alchemical sword cuts — not to destroy, but to separate. Calcination by iron is brutal, immediate, and necessary. Mars teaches that sometimes the only path through is through."

---

### ♃ JUPITER (Tin)

**Core identity:** Expansion, benevolence, abundance, fortune, the expansive principle, Sagittarius/Pisces ruler, Tin.

---

**Design 1 — Expansion**
- **Invaders:** Jupiter blocks are small when they first appear but expand 1 cell every 3 seconds. A fully expanded Jupiter is a 3×3 block filling 9 cells. Shoot early for less impact.
- **Tetris:** Jupiter pieces expand when they land — after 3 turns, they grow from 4-cell tetromino to 6-cell piece (adding 2 cells). Growth can be prevented by Earth adjacent blocks.

---

**Design 2 — Fortune (Random Bonus)**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Jupiter grants a random bonus — could be bonus XP, could be power-up, could be temporary shield. Jupiter is the fortune wheel: always good, never predictable.
- **Tetris:** Jupiter pieces grant a random bonus on placement — random selection from: extra time, bonus next piece, row clear, XP boost. Always beneficial, never the same twice.

---

**Design 3 — Abundance (Multiplication)**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Jupiter spawns 2 additional small Jupiter blocks adjacent to where it died. Those 2, when shot, spawn 1 each. Jupiter is generous with itself.
- **Tetris:** When a Jupiter piece is part of a cleared line, it adds one extra line to the count (a 1-line clear with Jupiter counts as 2, etc.). Jupiter multiplies line value.

---

**Design 4 — Inflation (Bloating)**
- **Invaders:** Jupiter blocks "inflate" nearby blocks — blocks within 2 cells grow larger (2× size). Larger blocks are harder to avoid but easier to shoot (bigger target). Jupiter bloats everything.
- **Tetris:** Jupiter pieces cause the current piece in play (and next piece) to become one size larger — 5-cell instead of 4-cell pieces. More powerful but harder to place.

---

**Design 5 — The Benevolent Wave**
- **Invaders:** Shooting Jupiter releases a generous wave — all player bullets currently in flight are multiplied (each bullet becomes 3 bullets). Jupiter helps the player.
- **Tetris:** When Jupiter piece clears a line, the next 3 pieces fall at half speed — Jupiter grants time and space, the luxury of deliberation.
- **Tome Entry (all Jupiter designs):** "Tin is soft, abundant, workable — the democratic metal. Jupiter rules abundance: the harvest, the treasury, the multiplied loaf. The alchemist who encounters Jupiter in a reaction finds it accommodating, generous, expressive. Jupiter expands everything it touches — for good or ill."

---

### ♄ SATURN (Lead)

**Core identity:** Limitation, death, time, the black phase (nigredo), melancholy, fixation to inertness, the heaviest metal, Saturn as Kronos/Time.

---

**Design 1 — Slowing (Time's Weight)**
- **Invaders:** Saturn blocks, when they appear, slow everything on screen by 30% for as long as they remain. Destroying Saturn immediately restores normal speed.
- **Tetris:** Saturn pieces cause all active timers to slow — drop interval increases by 20% while any Saturn piece remains on the board. More Saturn = slower pace. Clearing Saturn restores tempo.

---

**Design 2 — Nigredo (Blackening)**
- **Invaders:** Saturn blocks release a "black cloud" when shot — blocks caught in it lose their elemental identity temporarily, becoming grey/inert for 5 seconds. Inert blocks give no XP but are easily destroyed.
- **Tetris:** Saturn pieces "blacken" adjacent pieces — they lose their elemental color and become grey for 5 turns. Blackened pieces clear for no XP. Players must plan around Saturn's corrosive presence.

---

**Design 3 — Fixation (Heaviness)**
- **Invaders:** Saturn blocks cannot be moved by other blocks' effects. They are fixed where they appear and fall straight down, immune to Wave/Attraction effects. Saturn is unmovable.
- **Tetris:** Saturn pieces are heavy — they cannot be moved left/right once they begin falling. Players must rotate to manage them but cannot shift horizontally. Saturn tests acceptance of limitation.

---

**Design 4 — Mortality (Countdown)**
- **Invaders:** Saturn blocks have a countdown displayed on them (from 10). When countdown hits 0, they explode, dealing massive damage to the entire field. Saturn is the death clock.
- **Tetris:** Saturn pieces have a "life" countdown — they will crumble in 5 turns if not cleared. If they crumble, they turn into 4 individual inert grey cells that fill awkward spaces permanently. Saturn tests urgency.

---

**Design 5 — The Great Work Opposite (Lead → Gold)**
- **Invaders:** Saturn/Lead is the inverse of Sun/Gold. Saturn blocks, when destroyed by a weapon upgraded with Gold (Sun), transform into Sun blocks and join the player's side (turning helpful). Lead-to-Gold transmutation.
- **Tetris:** Saturn pieces, when cleared in the same line as a Sun piece, trigger a "Great Work" bonus — the line counts as completed twice (double clear) and grants maximum XP. The transmutation of Lead into Gold.
- **Tome Entry (all Saturn designs):** "Saturn is the beginning and the end. The nigredo — the black, stinking, mortifying stage — was Saturn's work. Lead was the lowest metal, Saturn the outermost planet, old age the saturnine time. But the alchemists knew: it is precisely the lowest, heaviest, most limited substance that holds the most potential. You cannot transmute gold. You can only transmute lead."

## THE TWELVE ZODIACAL PROCESSES

*Note: Zodiacal glyphs function as wave/phase modifiers in the current design. The following 5 designs each represent a different mechanical role for zodiacal processes — from passive wave modifier to active player ability.*

---

### ♈ ARIES — Calcination

**What Calcination means:** Burning to dry powder. The first alchemical operation. Reduction to essential form.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All blocks are brittle (1-hit kills) but give no XP until combined.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Aries blocks, when cleared, calcinate the row below — weakening those blocks.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Calcination to destroy your held piece for a 3-turn bonus fire rate.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** 3+ Fire clears in Aries wave doubles all subsequent Fire XP.
**Design 5 — Boss Encounter:** An Aries wave has one large "Calcination Boss" block — a 2×3 mass that must be fully surrounded before it can be destroyed.

**Tome Entry:** "Aries opens the zodiacal year with calcination: the first burning. The ram charges, the fire ignites, the body is reduced. What survives calcination is essential. What does not survive was not essential. This is the First Work."

---

### ♉ TAURUS — Dissolution

**What Dissolution means:** Dissolving ash in water. Solubilizing what calcination created.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All Water blocks have expanded radius — their dissolution effect covers 4 cells instead of 2.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Taurus blocks are slow and heavy but create "rich earth" when cleared — adjacent blocks gain +50% value.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Dissolution to transform one problematic solid piece into fluid blocks that fill any available gap.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Water+Earth pairs in Taurus wave produce Fertile Soil bonus blocks.
**Design 5 — Environmental:** The game board tilts 5° to the left during Taurus wave, affecting where pieces naturally fall.

**Tome Entry:** "After calcination comes dissolution — the ash is put in water, the soluble separates from the insoluble. Taurus governs patience and material reality. The bull does not hurry dissolution; the bull waits for the water to do its work."

---

### ♊ GEMINI — Separation

**What Separation means:** Separating dissolved components. Oil from water. Essential from accidental.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** Every block splits into two half-blocks when destroyed — more pieces to manage.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Gemini blocks always spawn in pairs. Can't have one without the other.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Separation to split your current piece into two separate single-cell blocks that fall independently.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Clearing a line with 5+ different elements in Gemini wave unlocks the "Separation Mastery" Tome entry.
**Design 5 — Visual:** During Gemini wave, each block shows its dual nature — a small secondary symbol showing what it would combine with best.

**Tome Entry:** "Gemini separates the combined. Two things that appeared to be one are revealed as distinct. The alchemist uses Gemini's sharpness — the twin blades — to identify components. Before you can recombine, you must know what you have."

---

### ♋ CANCER — Conjunction

**What Conjunction means:** Reuniting the separated elements in a new, purer form. The sacred marriage.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** Blocks placed adjacent to their opposite (Fire/Water, Earth/Air) create Conjunction combos worth 3× points.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Cancer blocks bind to adjacent blocks on landing — the bound group must be cleared as a unit.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Conjunction to merge your current piece with the held piece — creating a larger hybrid piece.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** A 4-line clear during Cancer wave triggers the "Holy Marriage" bonus — 5× score.
**Design 5 — Environmental:** Gravity is reduced during Cancer wave — all pieces fall 25% slower, enabling more deliberate placement.

**Tome Entry:** "Conjunction is the sacred marriage — the rejoining of the separated male and female principles into a new unified body. What was divided by calcination, dissolved by water, and separated by air is now brought together by Cancer's sheltering power. The crab carries its home on its back: Cancer is the first safe container."

---

### ♌ LEO — Fermentation

**What Fermentation means:** The introduction of a ferment/yeast — new life, transformation without fire.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All XP gains accumulate multiplier — each consecutive reaction without missing increases multiplier by 0.25×.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Leo blocks gradually transform adjacent blocks — blocks adjacent to Leo slowly gain Leo's properties over 3 turns.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Fermentation to transform the worst-fit piece on the board into a piece that perfectly fills the largest gap.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Placing 5 different elements on the same turn during Leo wave triggers "Wild Fermentation" — random cascade reactions across the board.
**Design 5 — Visual:** During Leo wave, the board has a warm golden glow and all blocks pulse with heat. Aesthetic amplification of the fermentation theme.

**Tome Entry:** "Leo is the summer king — the peak of heat, the longest day. Fermentation happens in warmth and time. The alchemist cannot hurry fermentation; she can only provide the conditions. Leo teaches patience at the apex of energy: not burning but ripening."

---

### ♍ VIRGO — Distillation

**What Distillation means:** Boiling and recondensing to purify. Refining the essence from the mixture.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** Line clears release a "distillate" — the purest block from the cleared line is returned to the queue. You get the best piece back.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Virgo blocks purify — blocks cleared in the same line as Virgo lose negative modifiers and become full-value.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Distillation to "purify" your queue — remove the worst upcoming piece and replace it with the best possible piece.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** 3 consecutive Virgo clears triggers "Perfect Distillation" — the next piece is guaranteed to be your chosen glyph's element.
**Design 5 — Mechanic:** During Virgo wave, a small "distillation column" appears on the side panel showing the current "purity" rating of the board (how many same-element pieces are grouped).

**Tome Entry:** "Virgo refines. The harvest, the sorting, the discernment of what is grain and what is chaff. Distillation rises upward — only the finest vapors ascend. Virgo's virtue is discrimination: knowing what belongs, what must be separated out, what is pure enough for the next operation."

---

### ♎ LIBRA — Coagulation

**What Coagulation means:** The congealing, crystallizing, or solidifying of the purified essence. Making permanent.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** The board "coagulates" — after 30 seconds, all unfilled gaps close by one cell from the sides. Balance becomes critical.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Libra blocks are the balance weight — when placed, they equalize the board (leftmost column gets a block if it's empty, or rightmost if that's emptier).
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Coagulation to freeze the board for 5 seconds — no blocks fall, allowing planning time.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** A perfectly symmetrical board (equal stack heights left/right) during Libra wave scores a "Perfect Balance" bonus.
**Design 5 — Environmental:** During Libra wave, the tilt mechanic is enhanced — the platform tilts by board asymmetry automatically, adding or subtracting from piece speed by column.

**Tome Entry:** "Libra governs the moment before solidification: the supersaturated solution that trembles on the edge of crystallizing. Coagulation is balance crystallized into permanence. The alchemist holds two opposing forces in suspension until the moment of equilibrium — then releases. What coagulates is finished."

---

### ♏ SCORPIO — Putrefaction

**What Putrefaction means:** The black, stinking, dying phase. Death before rebirth. The necessary corruption.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All blocks slowly "rot" — their XP value decreases over time. Clear quickly or lose value.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Scorpio blocks corrupt — blocks adjacent to Scorpio slowly turn black (lose identity). Corrupted blocks are cleared for reduced XP but enable bonus when cleared with uncorrupted blocks.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Putrefaction to sacrifice 3 blocks on the board — destroying them but triggering a "death bonus" that scores their value ×3.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Allowing the board to reach 80% full during Scorpio wave then clearing to 50% triggers "Death and Rebirth" — all remaining blocks become double value.
**Design 5 — Narrative:** Scorpio wave includes a slow ominous visual effect — the board color shifts toward deep purple/black, and all Tome entries discovered this wave are prefaced with "In the darkness of the nigredo..."

**Tome Entry:** "Putrefaction is the most feared and most necessary of the operations. The body must die, rot, stink, and blacken before it can be resurrected in purified form. The alchemist who flees putrefaction — who cannot tolerate the death phase — cannot complete the Work. Scorpio teaches the courage to let what was be finished."

---

### ♐ SAGITTARIUS — Fermentation (Continuation)

**What this phase means:** The second fermentation — the spirit rising in the form of alcohol, the inspired breath, the archer's upward aim.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All pieces fall 25% faster — the rising spirit accelerates everything.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Sagittarius blocks, when cleared, "launch" a bonus piece upward from the bottom of the board.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Sagittarius to launch your current piece upward — it clears any blocks it passes through on the way up (arrow through the board).
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Clearing a vertical column (rather than horizontal line) during Sagittarius scores "Archer's Line" — an alternative to Tetris.
**Design 5 — Mechanic:** During Sagittarius, a "zenith meter" fills with each clear. When full, all pieces glow briefly and score double for 5 seconds — the peak of fermentation.

**Tome Entry:** "The archer aims upward. Sagittarius rules the second fermentation — not the earthly rot of Scorpio but the rising spirit, the alcohol that lifts the soul from the dissolved body. The centaur is half beast, half sage: fermentation is both biological and divine."

---

### ♑ CAPRICORN — Fixation

**What Fixation means:** Making the volatile permanent. Binding what would escape. The philosopher's work made solid.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All blocks are fixed in place for 2 extra turns after landing — they cannot be affected by dissolution, dispersion, or other effects.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Capricorn blocks are anchors — they prevent all blocks within 2 cells from being moved by any effect.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Fixation to permanently "fix" any block on the board — it becomes immune to all modifiers and counts double in clears.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Building a 4-block-tall column of identical elements during Capricorn wave triggers "Fixed Tower" bonus — column counts as a vertical Tetris.
**Design 5 — Visual:** During Capricorn wave, cleared blocks leave crystalline residue for 3 turns — sparkling outlines where blocks were. The mountain goat's path remains visible.

**Tome Entry:** "Capricorn rules the winter solstice — the longest night, the moment when the year turns but has not yet begun. Fixation in winter: the cold that preserves, the frost that seals. The alchemist uses cold and time to fix what would otherwise evaporate. What is fixed can be kept, carried, used. Fixation is the completion of the volatile's education."

---

### ♒ AQUARIUS — Multiplication

**What Multiplication means:** Repeating the Work many times to amplify potency. The philosopher's stone multiplied.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** Every clear during Aquarius wave multiplies the next clear's value. Consecutive clears compound.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Aquarius blocks multiply — each clear that includes Aquarius adds an additional Aquarius block to the queue.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Multiplication to double the number of pieces currently falling (all effects, all scores × 2 for 10 seconds).
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Clearing 4 lines with the same element during Aquarius wave triggers "Element Mastery" — that element's Tome entry unlocks advanced cascade knowledge.
**Design 5 — Mechanic:** Aquarius introduces a "tincture meter" — each clear fills it slightly. When full, all subsequent clears are doubled until the meter empties. Cycle of multiplication.

**Tome Entry:** "Multiplication is the penultimate operation: the ability of the perfect Stone to multiply itself, to transmute not just one ounce of lead but a thousand pounds. The Water-Bearer pours without end — the vessel never empties. Aquarius teaches that true knowledge, once achieved, multiplies spontaneously."

---

### ♓ PISCES — Dissolution (Return to Prima Materia)

**What this phase means:** The completion and return — the final dissolution back into the undifferentiated potential of prima materia, readying the next cycle.

---

**Design 1 — Wave Modifier:** All blocks slowly dissolve — they fade gradually and disappear without scoring unless cleared in time. Everything returns to nothing.
**Design 2 — Block Type:** Pisces blocks are two fish — each Pisces block placed spawns a mirror block at the corresponding position on the other side of the board.
**Design 3 — Player Power:** Activate Dissolution Final to completely clear the board — all blocks are swept away — but score nothing. The slate is wiped clean.
**Design 4 — Combo Trigger:** Surviving Pisces wave with no pieces above row 15 (bottom third only) triggers "Return to Source" — all XP for the wave is tripled.
**Design 5 — Narrative/Endgame:** Pisces is the 12th wave. Completing it triggers a special Tome entry and begins a new zodiacal cycle at amplified difficulty. The Great Work recommences.

**Tome Entry:** "Pisces ends what Aries began. The two fish swim in circles — the alpha and omega, the serpent swallowing its tail. Final dissolution is not failure: it is completion. The prima materia waits again, ready to be worked. The alchemist who reaches Pisces has not finished — she has prepared the ground. The next Work will be deeper."

## THREE PARACELSIAN PRINCIPLES

### ♁ SULFUR

**Core identity:** The soul, combustibility, the capacity for transformation through heat, the masculine active principle.

---

**Design 1 — Volatility Modifier:** Sulfur blocks burn fast — they fall 40% faster than normal and clear twice as many lines if they ignite adjacent elements.
**Design 2 — Combustion Chain:** Sulfur blocks adjacent to Fire auto-ignite, clearing a 3×3 area around them.
**Design 3 — Soul Boost:** Activating Sulfur multiplies all Fire-related XP by 2× for 8 seconds.
**Design 4 — The Burning Soul:** Sulfur pieces that land without igniting become "frustrated" — they pulse increasingly until they finally ignite on their own (3-turn fuse).
**Design 5 — Tria Prima Combo:** Sulfur + Salt + Mercury in the same line clear triggers "Tria Prima" — the rarest combination — worth 5000 points and unlocking the most advanced Tome entries.

**Tome Entry:** "Sulfur is the soul of metals — the fiery principle, the combustible essence, the capacity for transformation. Paracelsus identified it as one of three fundamental principles. Without sulfur, a substance cannot transform. It is the fuel of the Great Work."

---

### ⚛ SALT

**Core identity:** The body, permanence, fixity, the principle of form, crystallization, the vessel in which Sulfur burns.

---

**Design 1 — Anchor:** Salt blocks are immovable once placed — they anchor everything around them.
**Design 2 — Purification:** Salt blocks absorb negative modifiers from adjacent blocks, becoming corrupted themselves but freeing the others.
**Design 3 — Crystal Formation:** 4 Salt blocks in a square form a Crystal — clears as a unit for massive bonus.
**Design 4 — Preservation:** Salt blocks prevent adjacent blocks from rotting or being corroded during Scorpio/Saturn waves.
**Design 5 — Tria Prima:** See Sulfur Design 5 — Salt is the second Tria Prima component.

**Tome Entry:** "Salt is the body — the crystallized, the fixed, the permanent. Paracelsus gave us three principles: Sulfur burns, Mercury flows, Salt endures. The body is salt: it outlasts the soul's fire and the spirit's motion. Salt preserves. Salt purifies. Salt is what remains when all else has been spent."

---

### ☿ MERCURY (Principle)

*Note: Mercury the Principle is distinct from Mercury the Planet, though they share a symbol. The principle is Hermes Trismegistus's Mercury — the medial, spiriting principle.*

---

**Design 1 — Mediator:** Mercury Principle blocks transform any adjacent block into a version that combines its properties with the other adjacent element.
**Design 2 — Flow:** Mercury Principle blocks are liquid — they "fill" any gaps they are adjacent to, splitting across two cells if needed.
**Design 3 — Communication:** All Mercury Principle blocks on the board are "linked" — what one does, all do simultaneously.
**Design 4 — Tria Prima:** See Sulfur Design 5 — Mercury is the third Tria Prima component.
**Design 5 — The Spirit:** Mercury Principle blocks, when cleared, add a "spirit bonus" to the next 5 clears — spirit rises, amplifying whatever follows.

**Tome Entry:** "Mercury is the spirit — neither fixed like Salt nor burning like Sulfur, but flowing, mediating, spiriting. Mercury connects body and soul, earth and sky, male and female. The alchemist who masters Mercury has grasped what cannot be grasped: the principle of transformation itself."

## RECOMMENDED UNIFIED DESIGN FRAMEWORK

### Core Design Rule: Each glyph's mechanic should embody its alchemical nature so precisely that the player can derive the alchemical meaning from the mechanic alone.

**Implementation Path:**
1. Start with top-ranked designs above
2. Ensure no two glyphs share a primary mechanic (each must be distinct)
3. Cascade unlocks should require witnessing glyphs in combination (not just individually)
4. Tome entries revealed only when the mechanic is activated — not passively

### Priority Cascade Combinations (Most Satisfying to Discover)

1. **Fire + Earth → Calcination** (Ash residue, Tome: Aries process)
2. **Water + Earth → Dissolution** (Mud formation, Tome: Taurus process)
3. **Air + Earth → Separation** (Dust, Tome: Gemini process)
4. **Fire + Water → Conjunction Attempt** (Steam, blocked conjunction, Tome: tension of Cancer)
5. **Sun + any element → Illumination** (Secret of element revealed)
6. **Saturn + any element → Nigredo** (Blackening, Tome: Putrefaction)
7. **Sulfur + Salt + Mercury in one clear → Tria Prima** (Rare, maximum XP)
8. **All 12 zodiac phases completed → Completion of the Great Work** (Final Tome entry)
