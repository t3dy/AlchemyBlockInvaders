# Spellbook of Alchemy — Complete Tome Entries

This document contains all the text entries that appear in the player's Tome (spellbook) as they progress through **Alchemy Block Invaders**. These entries are discoverable through gameplay and serve as the educational foundation of the game.

---

## PRINCIPLES

### The Principle of Fire 🔥

**Entry:** Automatically discovered at game start

Fire is the active principle of transformation. It consumes and transmutes all it touches, releasing what is bound within matter. When you strike a flame, you awaken its nature. Fire does not preserve—it destroys and creates in the same breath. It ascends, it spreads, it hungers. Yet without fire, nothing transforms. Without heat, the Great Work cannot proceed.

The alchemist learns first to respect the flame: to direct it without being consumed, to use its power to break down what seems solid, and to trust that from ash, something new may arise.

---

### The Principle of Water 💧

**Entry:** Automatically discovered at game start

Water is the solvent and the mirror. It moves where fire is rigid, and dissolves where fire burns. Water carries all properties within itself, yet binds to nothing permanently. It takes the shape of its vessel, reflects what gazes upon it, and finds its own level.

In alchemy, water represents the vessel state—the prima materia before transformation, the medium through which all work is accomplished. Water teaches patience. It asks nothing, yet shapes everything it touches. The alchemist must learn water's humility: to flow around obstacles rather than break through them.

---

### The Principle of Air 🌬️

**Entry:** Automatically discovered at game start

Air is the medium and the messenger. It rises, disperses, and spreads. Air carries the volatile essences that fire releases and water cannot hold. It is both seen and unseen, felt and intangible. Air is the breath, the wind, the spirit.

In the Great Work, air represents volatilization—the ascension of the subtle from the gross. Where fire acts with violence and water acts with patience, air acts with grace. It carries messages between realms, connects the earthly to the celestial, and reminds us that not all transformation requires force.

---

### The Principle of Earth 🌍

**Entry:** Automatically discovered at game start

Earth is the vessel and the foundation. It is heavy, stable, and contains. All transformations eventually settle into earth form. Without earth, there is no anchor; without grounding, the work becomes abstract fantasy.

Earth teaches the final lesson: that transformation must result in something real, something material, something you can hold. The alchemist's work is not merely intellectual—it manifests in the world. Earth is where all journeys end, and where all journeys must begin. It is the alpha and the omega, the vessel and the served.

## DISCOVERED REACTIONS

### Reaction: Fire + Water → Steam

**Entry:** Discovered upon first collision of fire element with water element

Flames meet the deluge. Water extinguishes fire, producing steam that rises and obscures. In this moment of apparent destruction, something new is born. The fire does not vanish—it transforms. The water does not simply quench—it ascends.

This is the first lesson the alchemist learns: that opposition need not be enmity. Fire and water, seemingly contrary, unite to create something neither could produce alone. Steam is weightless, boundless, impossible to contain. It rises toward the celestial realm, teaching us that matter can transcend its nature.

**Secondary Effect:** Mist Cloud

The steam spreads, creating a veil of obscurity. For a moment, vision is clouded and distinctions blur. This obscuring teaches an important truth: that transformation requires a period of chaos, a space where what was is no longer, and what shall be has not yet formed. In this fog, the work proceeds unseen.

---

### Reaction: Fire + Earth → Ash

**Entry:** Discovered upon first collision of fire element with earth element

Fire consumes the earth, leaving ash. Ash is dispersed by wind, becoming dust. In this reaction, earth—the stable, the founded, the immutable—yields to fire's hunger. What remains is not destruction, but residue: the essence extracted, the matter spent.

Ash is sacred in alchemy. It is the proof that transformation has occurred. The earth that seemed solid was always temporary. Fire reveals this truth: that all matter is fuel, that all forms are temporary vessels for the essential principle within.

**Secondary Effect:** Dust Dispersal

The ash rises and scatters, carried by unseen currents. Particles drift across the field, some carrying with them the imprint of fire. Where ash falls, it leaves traces. Some traces will ignite further reactions. Others will settle and wait. The alchemist learns that consequences extend beyond the immediate—that reactions ripple through time and space.

---

### Reaction: Fire + Air → Wildfire

**Entry:** Discovered upon first collision of fire element with air element

Fire ignites the air itself. The element feeds upon itself, growing wild and untamed. This is the most volatile reaction, the one the alchemist approaches with caution and awe. Fire and air should be controlled opposites, but together they create something beyond control.

In this reaction, fire finds infinite fuel. Air feeds the flame; the flame heats the air; the heated air rises and draws more cool air, which it then ignites. This is multiplication—the principle of geometric growth. The alchemist who encounters wildfire learns that some combinations do not resolve or balance. Some combinations amplify. Some combinations require wisdom and restraint.

**Secondary Effect:** Expansion

The block expands, glowing brighter, its heat radiating outward. Nearby blocks feel the warmth. The wildfire threatens to consume everything in its path. This teaches the final principle: that in alchemy, as in life, some forces must be respected, not mastered. The wise alchemist learns when to step back and let transformation proceed without interference.

---

### Reaction: Water + Earth → Mud

**Entry:** Discovered upon first collision of water element with earth element

Water binds with earth, forming mud. The mud settles and hardens. Two inert elements combine and create something dense and heavy. This is the nigredo—the blackening, the death phase of the Great Work, when all appears lost and confused.

Mud is the realm of fixation. It is heavy, slow, resistant to change. In alchemy, this represents the stage where materials coagulate, where the work seems to stall, where progress appears impossible. Yet the alchemist knows that stasis is temporary. What hardens can be broken. What seems fixed can be transformed again.

**Secondary Effect:** Stasis

The affected block becomes heavier, slower to descend. It resists destruction, requiring more effort to overcome. This teaches patience. Not all obstacles yield to direct force. Sometimes the work requires waiting for the right moment, the right reaction, the right allies. A block trapped in mud may only be freed by heat (fire) or dissolution (water again, applied differently).

---

### Reaction: Water + Air → Mist

**Entry:** Discovered upon first collision of water element with air element

Water rises into the air as mist, becoming ethereal and dispersed. Two volatile elements meet and create something between states—neither quite liquid nor quite vapor, neither quite visible nor quite invisible.

Mist represents ascension and rarefaction. Matter becomes spirit. The liquid and the gaseous combine to create subtlety. In the Great Work, this represents the albedo or whitening phase—where darkness gives way to clarity, where confusion transforms into illumination.

**Secondary Effect:** Mist Cloud

A veil of mist spreads across the field, obscuring vision but not preventing sight. Looking through mist, one sees everything hazily, imprecisely. This teaches the truth of subtle perception: that not all knowledge comes from sharp clarity. Sometimes we must learn to see through fog, to trust the shapes we perceive even when edges are blurred.

---

### Reaction: Air + Earth → Dust

**Entry:** Discovered upon first collision of air element with earth element

Air scatters the earth, breaking it into fine dust that floats away. The solid yields to the invisible. Earth's integrity is overcome not by fire's heat or water's flow, but by air's gentle, relentless persistence.

Dust is the ultimate state of division. It is matter reduced to its smallest components, each particle separate, dispersed. Yet dust is also life: soil, the foundation of growing things. When earth becomes dust, it becomes capable of nourishing. It becomes available. It becomes useful in ways solid earth cannot be.

**Secondary Effect:** Dispersion

Particles drift across the field, some falling, some rising. Each particle carries a fraction of the original block's nature. Where particles cluster, they may form temporary formations. Where they scatter, they become lost. This teaches the principle of separation—that to transform, matter must first be broken into constituent parts, and only then recombined into new forms.

## READING THE TOME

The Tome is meant to be read between rounds, during moments of reflection. Each entry teaches something about how the elements relate and transform. As you progress through the game and discover more reactions, your Tome grows. 

Over time, you may notice patterns:
- Fire always transforms; it never preserves
- Water always adapts; it never resists
- Air always disperses; it never fixes
- Earth always settles; it never rises

These are not rules written in stone. They are observations about the nature of the elements themselves. As you become more skilled, you may learn to use these natures deliberately, combining elements in specific sequences to achieve specific outcomes.

The ultimate goal is not to memorize the Tome, but to internalize its lessons so deeply that you no longer need to read it. You will begin to anticipate reactions, to understand the consequences of your choices before they occur. At that point, you have transcended being a player of the game and become an alchemist in truth.
