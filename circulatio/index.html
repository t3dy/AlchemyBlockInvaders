CIRCULATIO

A bounce engine whose physics is a metaphysics

You are the matter in the vessel. Circulatio is the operation in which the substance rises and falls, over and over, through the same apparatus, until it is fixed. You strike yourself around a course the way a cue strikes a ball, or you loft yourself over it. Every body you touch is dissolved; when one remains it becomes the mouth of the flask, and you must fall into it.

The point of the engine is the frame on the left. A ruleset is not a difficulty setting and not a skin — it is the physics. Gravity, rebound, grip and drag all come from the metaphysics you have selected, so a line that solves a course under Paracelsus may be impossible under Aristotle. Under the lettrist ruleset the global constants go slack and the law is whatever letter is written on the tile you are touching: press L and inscribe the course.

The dotted line is not an approximation. It is the real simulation run forward on a copy of the ball, so what you are shown is what will happen.
