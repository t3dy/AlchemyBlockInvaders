NOVA HEAT

Fruit of Life · Thirteen Circles · Seventy-Eight Lines

The page holds Metatron's Cube in pencil. Place spheres of prana into its thirteen circles. Any two charged circles light the line between them, and a lit line burns.

The Nova Mob comes in from the margin to drain the figure and reach the centre. Every one that gets there scorches the paper. Hold seven or more circles and the paper cools. Light all thirteen and the Mer-Ka-Ba turns — it clears the page and dumps the heat, but it is built out of the figure and leaves every circle dark behind it.
