SALAMANDRA

A flight through the burning body

The salamander is the creature that lives in fire, and Salamander is what Life Force was called before it crossed the ocean. You fly an alembic through the interior of the athanor, and the furnace is built.

The architecture is the puzzle. Every structure in here is masonry of a particular alchemical kind, and it answers to one thing only. Shoot it with the wrong matter and it will absorb the shot, harden against you, grow, ignite, or throw it straight back. Shoot it with what it yields to and it opens. Your gun is not one gun — it is eleven kinds of matter, and choosing among them is most of the game.
